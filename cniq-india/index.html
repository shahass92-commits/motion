04 · Sea & Air

Berth to runway, on the same record

Ocean and air freight are booked, tracked and handed over on the shipment record that already holds your road legs. One trail of documents, from gate-in to wheels-up.

  1. One shipment record

    Road, sea and air legs sit on the same record, with every handover and document attached.

  2. Live position

    GPS position, speed and geofence events reach your team as they happen.

  3. Checked before it’s billed

    Each leg is reconciled against its booking and locked before it reaches your invoice.

  4. Exceptions flagged early

    Delays, route changes and booking mismatches are flagged for a person to review.

  5. Every change on record

    Locked records are never overwritten. A change keeps the old and new value, who made it, when and why.

How a load moves

Six steps from booking to proof of delivery

  1. 01

    Book

    Your request is logged against your account and contract, with pickup, drop and cargo details.

  2. 02

    Assign

    A compliant vehicle and a verified driver are matched to the load. Permits and documents are checked.

  3. 03

    Dispatch

    The route, stops and handover points are planned and shared before the wheels turn.

  4. 04

    Track

    GPS, geofences and exception alerts follow the load in real time.

  5. 05

    Verify

    Distance, timings and handovers are reconciled against the booking and locked.

  6. 06

    Deliver

    Proof of delivery, trip sheet and invoice reach you as one linked package.
