How a load moves

Six steps from booking to proof of delivery

  1. 01

    Book

    Your request is logged against your account and contract, with pickup, drop and cargo details.

  2. 02

    Assign

    A compliant vehicle and a verified driver are matched to the load. Permits and documents are checked.

  3. 03

    Dispatch

    The route, stops and handover points are planned and shared before the wheels turn.

  4. 04

    Track

    GPS, geofences and exception alerts follow the load in real time.

  5. 05

    Verify

    Distance, timings and handovers are reconciled against the booking and locked.

  6. 06

    Deliver

    Proof of delivery, trip sheet and invoice reach you as one linked package.
